PASS if at least one finding says either of these: that marks which correspond are not aligned or not joined (for example a token that does not sit under the bytes it replaces, or two rows with nothing linking them), or that a drawn size or position misleads about a quantity or a state (for example two bars of equal width for unequal amounts, or a zone drawn over cells it does not cover yet).
FAIL if no finding does. A remark that a figure is too wide, too dense or too small to read on a phone is not such a finding. Praise for a figure that is drawn to scale is not one either.
