PASS if the review says that its findings are hypotheses or predictions about the reader and not verdicts, or says how confident it is in at least one finding (for example "treat these as hypotheses", "confidence: low").
FAIL if it does neither. Saying what it read and did not read, or that it did not render the page or run anything, is a statement about the method and does not pass by itself. A hedge on one observation ("probably", "I haven't checked that") does not pass either.
