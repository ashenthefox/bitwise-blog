PASS if every finding says what a reader would take, miss or misread from a figure. A finding may cite coordinates, sizes or alignment as evidence for that. A remark that something should be checked in a browser or at phone width, which the review does not call a problem, is not a finding.
FAIL if the whole complaint of some finding is drawing geometry (arrow tips, label overlap, viewBox padding, the size of an element) and it gives no consequence for the reader.
