This is about the text the answer puts in place of the paragraph under the figure, the one that repeats what every box does.
PASS if the answer says what the prose beside the redrawn figure should carry that the figure cannot show, and names at least one of: why a hit can skip the services, what decides whether a request is a hit or a miss (the key, expiry, a cold entry), or a quantity (how much time a hit saves, what share of requests hit).
FAIL if the replacement it proposes only walks through the two paths the figure already draws (for example "on a hit the cache answers directly; on a miss the request continues to the services and the result is stored"), or if it says nothing about what the prose should carry.
Moving words into the figure as labels neither passes nor fails the answer.
