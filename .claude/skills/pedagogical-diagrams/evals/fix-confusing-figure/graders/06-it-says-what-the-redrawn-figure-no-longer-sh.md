PASS if the answer tells the author to state in the figure's caption, or in a note on or next to the figure, that the redrawn figure leaves something out or draws several things as one (for example a caption line "Logging is omitted" or "the three gateways are drawn as one").
FAIL if it only lists what to cut or collapse, as advice to the author.
None of these passes by itself: a count on a box such as "×3"; advice to mention the removed parts in the body text or the paragraph under the figure, in another figure, or "if they matter"; a caption that says only what the figure shows or what a greyed box means.
