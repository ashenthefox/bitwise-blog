PASS if this holds of the answer: The plan says that the pages of the series link or refer to one another by title or by slug, and not by page or part number. One sentence that says so is enough, for example "pages link to each other by slug, not by number" or "the recap links to the first page by title". The plan's own headings and working notes may call the pages "page 1", "page 2" and "page 3": that must not fail it.
FAIL if the plan never says how one page names or links another, or says only that the pages link back, have previous and next links, or carry a "part N of 3" banner.
