PASS if the draft voices the reader's own questions or objections, in the reader's words (for example "wait, I never added that word, so why does it say maybe?"), at least twice, and at least one of them is about something the reader sees happen in the interactive. The author then answers each.
FAIL if the reader's voice appears once or not at all, or only as the author's rhetorical question ("why does this happen?") rather than a question attributed to the reader.
