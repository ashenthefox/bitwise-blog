PASS if the instruction before the interactive gives the reader at least one goal with a concrete, visible end state they can tell they have reached (for example "keep adding words until one you never added comes back as maybe" or "fill half the bits"), not only a description of what the controls do or an invitation to play.
FAIL if the reader is only told what the controls do, to try it, or what to notice, with no goal they can tell they have reached.
