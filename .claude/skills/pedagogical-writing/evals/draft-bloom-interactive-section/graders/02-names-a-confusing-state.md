PASS if, before or beside the interactive, the draft warns the reader about a state the interactive can show that may look like a mistake in the demo, and explains it. For example: adding a word sets fewer than 3 bits because two hash functions chose the same bit; or a word never added comes back "maybe".
A false positive explained only after a scripted demonstration counts only if the text tells the reader it may also happen to them while they play.
FAIL if no such state is anticipated, or it is mentioned without an explanation.
